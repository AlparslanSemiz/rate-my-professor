# User stories

From the team's planning board at the start of the course (the original was a sticky-note board). Roles: KIT student, user, professor, moderator. Items marked *optional* were nice-to-haves; *out of scope* was decided against.

## KIT student

- **Log in with my student mail**, so that I can verify that I am a student. *Given* I am on the login screen, *when* I log in or create an account, *then* the system verifies that I am a student.
- **See a professor's rating.** *Given* I am on a professor's profile, *when* I click the star rating, *then* I get a breakdown by discipline and module.
- **See a course rating.** *Given* I am on a course's profile, *when* I click the star rating, *then* I get a breakdown by discipline and professor.
- **Rate a lecture together with a professor in several disciplines.** *Given* I want to add a rating, *when* I click "add new rating", *then* I can pick a lecture and a professor and rate each discipline.
- **Search lectures** and see which professors held them in the past, with their ratings.
- **Search professors** and see which lectures they held in the past.
- **See and edit my own ratings** from a list on the start page.
- **See my SPO plan**: an overview of the core modules ("Stammmodule") of my major.

A rating is always for a (module, professor) pair; you reach it through the module or through the professor.

## Optional

- Read other students' comments on lectures and professors.
- Comment on professors and lectures, and on other comments.
- Up- and downvote comments, so that helpful ones are more visible.
- See official evaluation results next to the student ratings.
- Chat with other students in a room per professor.
- See pictures of professors.

## User

- **Report comments**, so that inappropriate content is reviewed and may be deleted.

## Professor

- **View my evaluation results and the comments on my lectures** (but not rate or comment).

## Moderator

- **Review reported comments**: remove them or block the user.

## Out of scope

- Rating tutors (Übungsleiter): not enough information to identify them; students can mention them in the free-text comments instead.
